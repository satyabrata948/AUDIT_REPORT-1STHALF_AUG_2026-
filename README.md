# AUDIT_REPORT-1STHALF_AUG_2026-
Sales Difference Audit &amp; Reconciliation – Meesho Supply Chain Operations
As part of my work in Supply Chain Analytics and Warehouse Operations, I regularly work with operational data such as picklists, dispatch quantities, product sales report(PSR), inventory, and courier transactions.

During an audit of the August 2026 Meesho operations data, I noticed a 53-unit Sales Difference in the reconciliation report.
Instead of simply adjusting the difference, I wanted to understand where the difference came from and what caused it.
This led me to perform a detailed reconciliation between the courier picklists and Product Sales Report (PSR).

The investigation finally helped me break down the 53-unit difference into:
43 units – Actual mismatch
10 units – Data-entry mistake

I started by checking all the picklists from 1st to 17th August for three courier partners:

1-Shadowfax
2-Delhivery
3-Volmo

I then reconciled the picklist quantities with the Product Sales Report (PSR) at the product level.

During this reconciliation, I found a 43-unit mismatch between the operational records and the PSR. After checking the remaining difference, I identified another 10 units as a data-entry mistake.

Why This Audit Was Important

At first glance, 53 units may look like a small number.
But in warehouse and supply chain operations, even a small quantity difference can create problems if it is not investigated properly.

A sales mismatch can eventually affect:
Sales Records → Inventory → Stock Reconciliation → Reporting → Operational Decisions

If the system does not reflect the actual movement of products, future inventory and sales reports can also become unreliable.
That is why I believe reconciliation is not just a reporting activity.
It is also a control mechanism for operational accuracy.

A variance is not the final answer. It is the beginning of the investigation.
